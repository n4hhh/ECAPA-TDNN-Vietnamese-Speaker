What the names do and do not encode
- The common aug_ prefix suggests augmentation or an augmented export, but a filename prefix alone does not prove either or name a method.
- train, train_small, test, and part are source-side group labels. The names alone do not establish an authoritative ML split protocol.
- shard-of-total and row values look like exporter/shard coordinates. They do not identify the source corpus, session, original utterance, noise source, speed factor, pitch factor, or other augmentation recipe.
- part is semantically ambiguous and is not treated as training data.
- train itself contains multiple shard-total regimes. Their labels must not be assumed to represent one homogeneous source.
